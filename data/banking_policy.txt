FICTIONAL BANK — CUSTOMER SERVICE POLICY

Document Version: 1.0
Document Type: Internal Customer Service Policy


1. ADDRESS CHANGE POLICY

Customers who want to change their residential address must provide:

- Government-issued photo identification
- Proof of the new address
- Completed address-change request form

Accepted proof of address includes a recent utility bill, government document, or bank statement.

Address changes must be reviewed by a bank employee before the change is finalized.


2. PHONE NUMBER CHANGE POLICY

Customers can request a phone number change through a bank branch or authorized customer service channel.

The customer must provide:

- Government-issued photo identification
- Existing account information
- The new phone number

A bank employee must verify the customer's identity before changing the phone number.


3. EMAIL ADDRESS CHANGE POLICY

Customers can request an email address change through an authorized customer service channel.

The customer must provide:

- Existing account information
- Identity verification
- The new email address

The change must be reviewed by a bank employee.


4. LOST OR STOLEN DEBIT CARD

If a customer loses a debit card or believes it has been stolen, the customer should contact the bank immediately.

The bank employee should:

1. Verify the customer's identity.
2. Temporarily block the card.
3. Record the incident.
4. Explain the card replacement process.

Lost or stolen card cases require human review.


5. DUPLICATE CARD CHARGE

If a customer reports that the same debit card transaction appears more than once, the customer service employee should:

1. Verify the customer's identity.
2. Collect the transaction date and amount.
3. Record the reported duplicate transaction.
4. Forward the case for transaction investigation.

The AI assistant must not decide whether the transaction is fraudulent.


6. UNAUTHORIZED CARD TRANSACTION

If a customer reports a transaction that they do not recognize or believe was unauthorized, the case must be escalated to a bank employee.

The customer service employee should:

- Verify the customer's identity.
- Record the transaction information.
- Temporarily block the card when appropriate.
- Escalate the case for investigation.

The AI assistant must not investigate or make a fraud determination.


7. PASSWORD RESET

Customers who forget their online banking password can request a password reset through the authorized password recovery process.

Identity verification is required before a password reset is completed.

Customer service employees must never request or record the customer's existing password.


8. ACCOUNT CLOSURE

Customers requesting account closure must contact an authorized bank employee.

The employee must:

- Verify the customer's identity.
- Check the account status.
- Explain any applicable closure requirements.
- Complete the account closure process according to bank procedures.


9. CUSTOMER COMPLAINTS

Customers can submit complaints through authorized customer service channels.

The employee should record:

- Customer information
- Complaint category
- Description of the issue
- Date of the complaint

Complaints requiring investigation must be forwarded to the appropriate bank team.


10. HUMAN REVIEW POLICY

The AI assistant provides information from the available bank policy documents.

The AI assistant must recommend human review when:

- The requested information is not found in the policy.
- The customer reports fraud or an unauthorized transaction.
- A decision requires access to confidential customer information.
- A bank employee must approve or investigate an action.

The AI assistant must not invent banking policies.

If information cannot be found in the provided policy document, the assistant should clearly say that the information was not found and recommend human review.
